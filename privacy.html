Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Gmail Cleaner is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own Gmail account. No other users can sign in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google's gmail.modify access), it reads message details such as sender, subject, labels and unsubscribe headers. It can move messages to Trash, add labels, and send unsubscribe emails that mailing lists request.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to sort the owner's mail into junk and keep.
  • Sign-in tokens, sender lists and logs are stored on the owner's own private server.
  • Sender details and subject lines may be sent to Anthropic's Claude, under the owner's own Claude account, to help classify unclear senders.
  • Data is never sold, shared with advertisers or used for any other purpose.

Gmail Cleaner's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
