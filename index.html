Gmail Cleaner

Gmail Cleaner is a private, personal tool. It is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Gmail account.

It finds advertising and junk mail, unsubscribes from mailing lists where the sender supports it, and moves junk to Gmail's Trash (where it can be restored for 30 days). It runs on the owner's own server. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's mail.
